ctrl Network Firewall (this page)
Runs on our side of the network, outside your VPS. It can block inbound traffic before your server receives it.
OS firewall (inside your VPS)
Your second layer, using tools such as UFW, firewalld, or iptables when you have one set up. Use it for finer control this page does not cover, for example source-IP limits or outbound filtering.

Frequently asked questions
More questions about a RackNerd VPS special? Contact us.
Can I renew a RackNerd VPS special at the same rate?
Yes. These RackNerd VPS specials are lifetime recurring. You can renew at the same rate for as long as you keep the service.
Do RackNerd plans include a free Clientexec license?
Yes. RackNerd includes a free Clientexec license with any active VPS, shared hosting, reseller hosting, or dedicated server plan. If you buy directly from Clientexec, the regular price for a branded license is $16.95/month. Open a support ticket to request yours.
Clientexec combines billing, a help desk, and customer relationship management (CRM). If you sell hosting, you can automate account setup with a supported control panel and invoice customers at renewal. You can also bill for a one-time design project or a monthly maintenance plan and keep each client’s payments and support tickets in one place.
What is RackNerd ctrl?
ctrl is RackNerd’s VPS control panel. Reboot your VPS, reinstall its operating system, access the console, manage network firewall rules, and install one-click apps from one place. Try the interactive demo above.
What if I want to deploy something that is not in the ctrl one-click apps catalog?
You have full root access. You can install your own applications, packages, and services over SSH, subject to the resources of your plan and our acceptable use policy.
Can I self-host apps on a RackNerd VPS special?
Yes. Host your own applications, websites, databases, and automation tools. Choose a plan with enough memory, storage, and CPU for the software you want to run.
Can I run Docker, WordPress, or my own stack on a RackNerd VPS special?
Yes. KVM virtualization and full root access let you install Docker, WordPress, and your preferred software stack. You are responsible for configuring and maintaining your software.
Which operating systems can I install on a RackNerd VPS special?
Choose from the available Linux templates in ctrl, including Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux, and Rocky Linux. The available templates are shown in the reinstall menu.
Do RackNerd VPS specials include full root access and an IPv4 address?
Yes. Every VPS special shown here includes full root access and one dedicated IPv4 address.
Where can I deploy a RackNerd VPS special, and how fast is it ready?
Choose from the available datacenter locations for your plan at checkout. VPS services are automatically provisioned after successful payment.
Can I run AI tools like OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, or n8n on a RackNerd VPS special?
Yes. ctrl includes one-click apps for AI tools and automation. Check each app’s requirements and choose a VPS with enough resources. Some apps also require a separate API key or subscription.
Is a RackNerd VPS special managed?
These VPS plans are self-managed. You control the operating system and your applications; RackNerd maintains the underlying infrastructure and provides 24/7 support.

Dedicated Server questions
Need help with a configuration? Contact us.
How long does dedicated server deployment take?
Dedicated servers are physical machines built specifically for you. Setup is not instant and provisioning may take up to 2–3 days. For instant setup, choose our VPS plans.
Which datacenter locations are available?
The Dual Xeon L5520, E5-2660, and E5-2683 V4 deploy in Seattle. The Intel Xeon E3-1230 V2 is provisioned at the first available US datacenter based on inventory. Contact sales before ordering if you need a different location.
Can I customize the server configuration?
Contact sales with your CPU, memory, storage, bandwidth, and IP requirements. Our team can help you find an available configuration or discuss a custom solution.
Are the server resources dedicated to me?
Yes. The physical server’s processor, memory, and storage are dedicated to your workloads.
Can I reinstall the operating system later?
Contact support to arrange an operating system reinstall and confirm the installation requirements. Back up your data before a reinstall.
Can you install an operating system or custom ISO?
We can help with Linux, Windows, FreeBSD, and custom installation requirements. Contact sales to confirm compatibility, licensing, and any installation needs before ordering.
Do RackNerd plans include a free Clientexec license?
Yes. RackNerd includes a free Clientexec license with any active VPS, shared hosting, reseller hosting, or dedicated server plan. If you buy directly from Clientexec, the regular price for a branded license is $16.95/month. Open a support ticket to request yours.
Clientexec combines billing, a help desk, and customer relationship management (CRM). If you sell hosting, you can automate account setup with a supported control panel and invoice customers at renewal. You can also bill for a one-time design project or a monthly maintenance plan and keep each client’s payments and support tickets in one place.
